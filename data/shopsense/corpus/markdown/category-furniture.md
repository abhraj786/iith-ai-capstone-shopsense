# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

**KARTWAY OPERATIONS MANUAL**
**ADDENDUM F-04: FURNITURE CATEGORY POLICY**
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026
**Applicability:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Support Agents, Logistics Operations, and Dispute Resolution Teams

---

### 1.0 RETURN WINDOW AND INITIATION

*   **1.1 Standard Return Window:** The return window for all items within the Furniture category is strictly **7** calendar days from the confirmed date of delivery.
*   **1.2 Defective or Wrong Item Reporting:** Any item delivered damaged, incorrect, or defective must be reported to Kartway Support within **7** calendar days of delivery. Reports exceeding this window shall be rejected for category-specific freight remediation.
*   **1.3 Expiry of Window:** Return requests initiated on day **8** or later from delivery shall be automatically blocked by the order management system, barring active statutory escalations managed exclusively by the Tier-3 Legal Escalations Team.

### 2.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND HANDLING CLASSIFICATIONS

*   **2.1 Assembled Status:** For the purposes of inspection and restocking classification, an assembled item explicitly counts as an opened item. Unassembly by the customer is not required prior to collection, but structural alterations voids return eligibility under Section 4.
*   **2.2 Change of Mind Condition:** An opened item returned due to a change of mind must be complete with all structural components, hardware, and assembly manuals, though original packaging is not mandatory for return acceptance.
*   **2.3 Hazardous and Perishable Handling:** Furniture containing integrated electrical components, pressurized gas lifts (e.g., hydraulic office chairs), or chemical finishes must be inspected by the pickup handler for leakage or exposed wiring prior to loading. Perishable upholstery (leather treated with organic oils) must be sealed in protective plastic sleeves provided by the collection agent to prevent transit mold.

### 3.0 FEES, DEDUCTIONS, AND RESTOCKING CHARGES

*   **3.1 Change of Mind Restocking Fee:** An opened item returned for a change of mind carries a mandatory restocking fee of **20**% of the total amount paid by the customer.
*   **3.2 Return Pickup Fee:** The return pickup fee for a change of mind return is **499** rupees. This amount shall be deducted directly from the final refund disbursement.
*   **3.3 Defective Item Fee Waiver:** A damaged, wrong, or defective item reported within the **7**-day window is refunded in full with **0** fees deducted (both restocking and pickup fees are waived entirely).

### 4.0 LOGISTICS AND PICKUP SCHEDULING

*   **4.1 Handler Requirement:** Furniture return pickup logistics require exactly **2** handlers present at the collection premises due to volumetric weight and safety mandates. Single-handler pickups are prohibited.
*   **4.2 Booking Lead Time:** Pickup for furniture returns must be booked at least **48** hours ahead of the proposed collection slot. Same-day or **24**-hour emergency pickups are unavailable for this category.

### 5.0 WARRANTY POSITION

*   **5.1 Listing-Dependent Duration:** Furniture products carry the exact warranty period shown on their own individual product listing. Valid warranty durations are strictly limited to **0**, **6**, **12**, or **24** months.
*   **5.2 Commencement Date:** The warranty coverage period is counted strictly from the confirmed delivery date recorded in the Kartway shipping system.
*   **5.3 Exclusions:** Items listed with a warranty period of **0** months are ineligible for post-delivery manufacturer defect claims through Kartway after the expiration of the standard **7**-day return window.

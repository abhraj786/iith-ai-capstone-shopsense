# Category Policy Addendum: Apparel

# KARTWAY OPERATIONS POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM 4C: APPAREL CATEGORY RULES
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Version:** 4.2
**Applies To:** All Tier 1 and Tier 2 Support Agents, Dispute Resolution Specialists

---

### 4C.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This addendum establishes the mandatory operational rules for handling customer service requests, returns, refunds, and warranty inquiries within the Apparel category on the Kartway marketplace. Where the general marketplace policy conflicts with this addendum, the rules set forth in this addendum supersede all other documentation.

### 4C.2 RETURN WINDOW
4C.2.1 The standard return window for all items listed in the Apparel category is exactly **45 days** from the verified delivery date recorded by the carrier.
4C.2.2 Requests initiated after 45 days from delivery shall be automatically rejected by the support system, with no agent discretion permitted.

### 4C.3 CATEGORY-SPECIFIC CONDITION REQUIREMENTS
4C.3.1 To be eligible for a return under the standard return window, apparel items must meet the following mandatory condition criteria:
* **Item Status:** Items must be completely unworn and unwashed.
* **Tag Status:** Original manufacturer and brand tags must remain fully attached to the item.
* **Innerwear and Swimwear:** Innerwear and swimwear items are strictly non-returnable unless received by the customer in a defective condition.

### 4C.4 ORIGINAL PACKAGING
4C.4.1 Original packaging (such as branded boxes, poly bags, or tissue paper) is **not mandatory** for processing an apparel return. Agents shall not refuse a return solely due to the absence of original packaging, provided the item meets the condition requirements stated in Section 4C.3.

### 4C.5 RESTOCKING FEES AND RETURN SHIPPING
4C.5.1 There is **zero restocking fee** applied to any returned item within the Apparel category.
4C.5.2 The return shipping pickup fee for a change of mind return is **0.00 currency units** (the return pickup for a change of mind is completely free for the customer).

### 4C.6 DAMAGED, WRONG, OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
4C.6.1 If a customer receives a damaged, incorrect, or defective apparel item, the issue must be formally reported to Kartway customer support within **45 days** of the delivery date.
4C.6.2 Validated claims for damaged, wrong, or defective items shall be refunded in full (**100% refund** of the purchase price and initial shipping costs) with **zero fees** deducted.

### 4C.7 HAZARDOUS AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
4C.7.1 Standard apparel items do not carry hazardous or perishable classifications. In the event an apparel item is contaminated with biological fluids or chemical residue prior to return, agents must classify the item as a biohazard, refuse the return, issue a permanent account warning to the customer, and dispose of the item in accordance with local municipal waste protocols without issuing a refund.

### 4C.8 WARRANTY POSITION
4C.8.1 **No warranty applies** to any item within the Apparel category.
4C.8.2 Any warranty period or guarantee displayed on a product listing within this category is classified strictly as a listing error and provides **zero cover** or entitlement to repair, replacement, or post-return compensation. Agents must direct customers to Section 4C.8.1 when addressing warranty claims.

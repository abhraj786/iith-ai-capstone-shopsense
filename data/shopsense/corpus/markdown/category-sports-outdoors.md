# Category Policy Addendum: Sports & Outdoors

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM: SPORTS & OUTDOORS
**Document ID:** POL-SO-409
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Applies To:** All Tier 1, Tier 2, and Specialist Support Agents handling Kartway Marketplace transactions in the Sports & Outdoors category.

---

### SECTION 1: RETURN WINDOW AND ELIGIBILITY

**1.1 Standard Return Window**
The return window for all items categorized under Sports & Outdoors is exactly 30 calendar days from the verified delivery date. No extensions, grace periods, or manager overrides shall be granted beyond the 30-day limit, aligning with the standard marketplace window.

**1.2 Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**
Any item received in a damaged, incorrect, or defective state must be reported by the customer within 30 days of delivery. Such items qualify for a full refund of the amount paid with zero restocking fees and zero return shipping deductions.

---

### SECTION 2: CHANGE OF MIND RETURNS AND FEES

**2.1 Change of Mind Conditions**
Customers returning an item due to a change of mind within the 30-day return window are subject to specific deductions handled automatically by the Kartway refund and shipping systems.

**2.2 Restocking Fee**
An opened item returned for a change of mind carries a mandatory restocking fee of exactly 12% of the amount paid by the customer. This fee is automatically calculated and retained from the refund total.

**2.3 Return Pickup Fee**
For all change of mind returns requiring a carrier pickup, a return pickup fee of exactly 99 rupees is assessed and deducted directly from the final refund amount.

---

### SECTION 3: CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING

**3.1 General Item Condition**
Returned items must be complete with all included accessories, manuals, and components unless otherwise specified in this addendum.

**3.2 Packaging Requirements**
Original packaging is **not** mandatory for returns within the Sports & Outdoors category. Support agents shall not reject a return request solely due to the absence of the manufacturer's original box or outer carton.

---

### SECTION 4: CATEGORY-SPECIFIC HANDLING AND RESTRICTIONS

**4.1 Protective Gear**
Helmets and other protective gear (including climbing harnesses, padded guards, and safety restraints) are strictly non-returnable and will not be accepted back once they have been used or worn.

**4.2 Hazardous Materials and Consumables**
Gas canisters, camping fuel, and pressurized containers are strictly non-returnable under all circumstances due to hazardous transport regulations. Support agents must advise customers to dispose of these items locally in accordance with municipal waste guidelines.

---

### SECTION 5: WARRANTY POSITION

**5.1 Manufacturer and Listing Warranties**
Products within the Sports & Outdoors category do not carry a blanket marketplace warranty. Instead, products carry the specific warranty period explicitly shown on their individual product listing page.

**5.2 Warranty Duration and Calculation**
Permitted warranty periods on listings are strictly limited to one of the following durations:
* 0 months (None)
* 6 months
* 12 months
* 24 months

The warranty count begins strictly on the verified delivery date and runs for the exact number of months stated on the listing at the time of purchase. Expired warranties must be redirected to the manufacturer's authorized service partner.

# Category Policy Addendum: Groceries

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY MANUAL
## ADDENDUM GR-04: GROCERIES CATEGORY OPERATIONS
**Owner:** Customer Operations Policy Team
**Applies To:** Tier 1–3 Support Agents, Dispute Resolution Specialists, Returns Processing
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025

---

### GR-04.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document establishes the binding operational rules for customer service agents handling inquiries, disputes, and refund requests within the Kartway Groceries marketplace category. Where this addendum conflicts with general marketplace guidelines, Addendum GR-04 supersedes all general provisions.

---

### GR-04.2 RETURN WINDOW AND CHANGE-OF-MIND RESTRICTIONS
1. **Non-Returnable Status:** The Groceries category is classified as entirely non-returnable. No change-of-mind returns are accepted under any circumstances.
2. **Change-of-Mind Requests:** Support agents must reject all customer requests for returns, exchanges, or refunds based on buyer remorse, incorrect personal selection, or taste preference. Agents must cite Policy GR-04.2(1) in all rejection communications.

---

### GR-04.3 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND ORIGINAL PACKAGING
1. **Condition Standard:** Due to health and safety regulations, standard physical condition assessments do not apply to accepted grocery claims.
2. **Packaging Mandate:** Original packaging is **not mandatory** for the processing of valid claims under this category. Customers are not required to retain outer boxes, plastic wraps, or manufacturer seals to qualify for resolution under GR-04.5.

---

### GR-04.4 RESTOCKING FEES
1. **Fee Structure:** A restocking fee of **0%** applies to all transactions within the Groceries category.
2. **Deductions Prohibited:** Agents are strictly prohibited from deducting return shipping, handling, or restocking charges from any customer refund issued in this category.

---

### GR-04.5 DAMAGED, WRONG, OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
1. **Reporting Deadline:** Any claim concerning a damaged, wrong, or defective item must be reported by the customer within **2 days** of the recorded delivery timestamp. Claims submitted after the expiration of the 2-day window must be automatically rejected by support agents.
2. **Refund Entitlement:** Valid claims reported within the 2-day window shall be refunded in **full** with **no fee** applied.
3. **Verification Method:** Claims for a damaged, expired, or wrong item must be resolved based on a clear photographic submission provided by the customer. Physical return of the item is **never** required.

---

### GR-04.6 HAZARDOUS AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
1. **Collection Prohibition:** Perishable and chilled items are **never** collected by Kartway logistics partners or third-party sellers under any operational scenario.
2. **Disposal Guidance:** Customers instructed to discard perishable, chilled, damaged, or expired items must do locally; agents must not generate return shipping labels for these items.

---

### GR-04.7 WARRANTY POSITION
1. **No Warranty Coverage:** **No warranty** applies to any product listed within the Groceries category.
2. **Listing Error Protocols:** Any warranty period, duration, or guarantee displayed on a product listing within this category constitutes a **listing error** and provides **zero cover** or legal obligation for Kartway or the merchant. Agents must inform inquiring customers that consumable grocery items are sold strictly as-is without ongoing manufacturer or marketplace guarantees.

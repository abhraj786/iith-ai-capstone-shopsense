# Kartway Operations Policy Addendum: TOYS-202X
**Category Owner:** Arvind Mehta, Head of Category Operations (Toys & Juveniles)
**Applies To:** All Tier-1 and Tier-2 Customer Support Agents, Resolution Specialists, and Return Processing Hubs
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2026
**Supersedes:** Version 4.1 (Toys Category)

---

## 1. Return Window and Timeframes

**1.1 Standard Return Window**
The return window for all items listed under the Toys category is strictly 20 days from delivery. Support agents must reject any return request initiated on or after day 21 from the recorded carrier delivery timestamp.

**1.2 Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**
Any toy received in a damaged, wrong, or defective condition must be reported by the customer within 20 days of delivery. Reports exceeding 20 days shall be redirected to the manufacturer warranty process outlined in Section 6.

---

## 2. Condition Requirements and Packaging

**2.1 Original Packaging Mandate**
Original packaging is mandatory for all toy returns processed through Kartway. The item must be returned inside its original manufacturer box, blister pack, or branded outer carton.

**2.2 Packaging Integrity**
If the original packaging is destroyed, defaced, or missing, the return shall be rejected by the Kartway return processing hub and sent back to the customer at their expense, with no refund issued.

---

## 3. Change of Mind Returns and Deductions

**3.1 Restocking Fee**
An opened item returned for a change of mind carries a restocking fee of 5% of the amount paid. This fee is automatically calculated by the billing engine and withheld from the final refund total.

**3.2 Return Pickup Fee**
The return pickup fee for a change of mind return is 99 rupees, which is deducted directly from the refund amount alongside the restocking fee specified in Clause 3.1.

**3.3 Defective Item Exception**
A damaged, wrong, or defective item returned under Clause 1.2 is refunded in full with no fee (no restocking fee and no return pickup fee applied).

---

## 4. Hazardous Handling and Safety Rules

**4.1 Button Cell Batteries**
Toys containing button cells are packed so the battery compartment is taped shut. Support agents advising customers on return transit requirements must instruct them to ensure the factory-applied tape over the button cell compartment remains intact during repackaging to prevent short-circuits.

**4.2 Product-Safety Reports**
A small part that comes loose is classified as a product-safety report. If a customer reports a detached small part from a toy during normal use or upon unboxing, support agents must immediately tag the ticket as `SAFETY-CRITICAL-TOYS`, halt standard troubleshooting, and route the case to the Kartway Product Safety Team within 2 hours.

---

## 5. Perishable Handling Rules

**5.1 Perishable Toys**
Toys containing organic or perishable compounding materials (such as dough, edible components, or plant-based growth kits) cannot be returned once unsealed, regardless of the 20-day return window, due to biological degradation risks. Unopened perishable toys may be returned within the standard 20-day window subject to the terms in Section 3.

---

## 6. Warranty Position

**6.1 Listing-Dependent Warranty**
Kartway does not provide a blanket warranty for toys. Products carry the warranty period shown on their own listing (none, 6, 12, or 24 months), counted from the delivery date.

**6.2 Warranty Claims Post-Return Window**
For warranty claims raised after the 20-day return window but within the active warranty period displayed on the listing, support agents must direct the customer to the authorised service center partner listed on the product detail page or initiate a manufacturer repair routing. Kartway does not issue direct refunds for items failing outside the 20-day return window.

# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

**KARTWAY CUSTOMER OPERATIONS POLICY HANDBOOK**
**Document Ref:** COP-ADD-CAT-ELEC-04
**Category:** Consumer Electronics & Computing Hardware
**Policy Owner:** Malini Sen, Lead Specialist, Returns & Escalations
**Effective Date:** 01 November 2024

---

### 1.0 Return Window
* **1.1 Standard Eligibility Window:** Customers must initiate all standard return requests for items under the Electronics category within exactly **15 days from delivery**.
* **1.2 Expiration:** The return interface locks at 23:59:59 on the 15th calendar day following documented carrier delivery. Support agents are barred from overriding this cutoff for change-of-mind claims.

---

### 2.0 Condition Requirements & Packaging Integrity
* **2.1 Original Packaging Mandate:** Original packaging is **mandatory** for every return in this category. The outer manufacturer box, serialized barcodes, internal trays, inserts, protective films, user manuals, and bundled hardware accessories must be present.
* **2.2 Data Erasure:** Personal user data, factory-locked credentials (including BIOS passwords, biometric locks, cloud account linkages, and personal PINs) must be cleared prior to handover. An agent may not authorize a return if a device remains remotely or physically locked to a user profile.

---

### 3.0 Change-of-Mind Returns and Associated Deductions
* **3.1 Opened Package Restocking Fee:** If a change-of-mind return involves an item whose manufacturer packaging seal has been broken or packaging has been unsealed, Kartway applies a restocking fee of **15% of the amount paid**. This sum is automatically calculated by the billing system and deducted from the gross credit.
* **3.2 Unopened Returns:** Items returned in an unopened, factory-sealed state for change of mind incur no restocking fee.
* **3.3 Return Pickup Fee:** Every change-of-mind return incurs a return pickup fee of **99 rupees**, which is deducted directly from the refund total prior to disbursement.
* **3.4 Fee Calculation Sequence:** For an unsealed change-of-mind unit:
  $$\text{Net Refund} = \text{Amount Paid} - (\text{Amount Paid} \times 0.15) - 99 \text{ rupees}$$

---

### 4.0 Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Deliveries
* **4.1 Notification Window:** A customer who receives an item that is damaged in transit, physically defective on arrival, or incorrect (mismatched SKU or specification) must report the issue within **15 days of delivery**.
* **4.2 Refund Settlement:** Validated claims under Clause 4.1 are **refunded in full with no fee**.
* **4.3 Fee Exemptions:** Neither the **15%** restocking fee nor the **99 rupees** pickup fee applies to shipments covered under this section. Reverse shipping charges are absorbed fully by Kartway.

---

### 5.0 Hazardous Materials & Battery Logistics
* **5.1 Lithium Battery Reverse Logistics:** Any item containing or packaged with lithium-ion or lithium-metal cells must be routed through certified surface logistics channels. Agents must set the carrier routing protocol to **surface transport only**; air haulage is prohibited under carrier hazmat rules.
* **5.2 Thermal and Physical Battery Hazards:** A battery that is visibly swollen, bulging, emitting smoke, or exhibiting extreme overheating is classified as a hazardous incident.
* **5.3 Hazard Protocol Override:** Any occurrence defined in Clause 5.2 must be processed as a **product-safety report, never a routine return**.
  * **5.3.1 Exclusion from Return Transport:** Physical reverse pickup of the defective battery is strictly prohibited to prevent transit fires.
  * **5.3.2 Escalation Assignment:** The agent must log an emergency ticket to the Kartway Safety Incident Desk (Queue: `SEC-BAT-ALERT`) and instruct the customer on local hazardous waste disposal.

---

### 6.0 Warranty Coverage
* **6.1 Warranty Assignment:** Items within this category do not carry a unified blanket coverage duration. Instead, products carry the warranty period shown on their own listing.
* **6.2 Permitted Warranty Terms:** The catalog accepts only four definitive warranty terms: **none**, **6 months**, **12 months**, or **24 months**.
* **6.3 Warranty Inception:** The warranty window begins on the **delivery date** confirmed by the carrier, not on the original order placement or dispatch date. Claim verification relies solely on the recorded delivery timestamp.

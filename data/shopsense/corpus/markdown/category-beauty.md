# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

# KARTWAY OPERATIONAL POLICY HANDBOOK
## ADDENDUM 4.2: BEAUTY CATEGORY PROTOCOLS
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Document Owner:** Priya Nair, Head of Customer Operations Policy
**Applicability:** All Tier 1 and Tier 2 Support Agents, Logistics Exceptions Team

---

### 1.0 PURPOSE AND PRECEDENCE
1.1 This addendum governs all customer service operations, returns processing, and dispute resolution for products listed under the Beauty category on the Kartway marketplace.
1.2 The figures and rules set forth in this addendum supersede any conflicting general provisions in the Master Operations Manual. Agents must apply the exact parameters detailed herein.

### 2.0 RETURN WINDOWS AND ELIGIBILITY
2.1 **Standard Return Window:** The return window for all items within the Beauty category is exactly **10** days from the recorded delivery date. Return requests initiated on day **11** or later shall be automatically rejected by the system.
2.2 **Change of Mind Restrictions:** An opened item cannot be returned for a change of mind under any circumstances.
2.3 **Restocking Fees:** Because opened items are ineligible for change-of-mind returns, no restocking fee exists for this category.
2.4 **Change of Mind Logistics Fee:** Where a customer initiates an eligible unopened change-of-mind return, a return pickup fee of **99** rupees shall be deducted from the final refund amount.

### 3.0 CONDITION REQUIREMENTS AND PACKAGING
3.1 **Original Packaging:** Original packaging is mandatory for all Beauty category returns. Items received without their original manufacturer packaging, or with damaged packaging that compromises product integrity, shall be rejected and returned to the customer at their expense.
3.2 **Seal Integrity:** The seal must be intact upon return inspection. Any item with a broken, tampered, or missing safety seal shall be deemed used and ineligible for refund.

### 4.0 DAMAGED, WRONG, OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
4.1 **Reporting Window:** A damaged, wrong, or defective item must be reported within **10** days of delivery. Reports exceeding this **10**-day threshold shall not be eligible for claims under this clause.
4.2 **Refund Terms:** Items validated under Clause 4.1 shall be refunded in full with no fee and no pickup deduction applied.

### 5.0 HAZARDOUS AND PERISHABLE HANDLING RULES
5.1 **Flammable Classification:** Aerosols, perfume, and nail polish are classified as flammable goods.
5.2 **Transport Restrictions:** Flammable goods listed in Clause 5.1 are collected by surface transport only. Air transport bookings for these items are strictly prohibited. Agents must ensure reverse logistics tickets specify ground-only transit.

### 6.0 WARRANTY POSITION
6.1 **Category Exclusion:** No warranty applies to the Beauty category.
6.2 **Listing Errors:** A warranty period shown on a listing in this category is classified strictly as a listing error and gives no cover. Agents must not honor manufacturer or seller warranties for items within this category and must direct aggrieved customers to the brand owner directly if service is sought past the **10**-day return window.
